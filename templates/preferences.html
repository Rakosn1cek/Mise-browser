Privacy & Protection

Permanent Private Workspace Isolates navigation into an in-memory session partition with subtle rose tint.
WebRTC IP Handling & Leak Protection Controls WebRTC address disclosure and STUN candidate gathering across network interfaces.
DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) Encrypts DNS queries directly to a privacy-focused resolver, bypassing local network and ISP inspection.
Operational Security Advisory: When enabled, queries travel directly to the selected third party resolver under their independent privacy terms. DoH encrypts domain lookups but does not guarantee anonymity, disguise your origin IP address, or bypass destination IP blocking. Use is at your sole discretion and risk.
Active Site Data Clear session cache or surgical cookies for the current domain.
Trusted Sites (Banking / Shopping) Sites listed here skip fingerprint randomisation, tracker blocking and permission blocking, since banks and checkout flows treat those protections as fraud signals. Every other site keeps full protection. One domain per line, e.g. yourbank.co.uk (subdomains are covered automatically). Applies immediately, no restart needed.

Granular Permissions Vault

Local POSIX 0600 Vault
Zero-Trust Architecture: Persistent workspaces track granular permissions per domain. Private Profile (MisePrivateProfile) enforces an unconditional sensor block to prevent device fingerprinting and physical location tracking.

Diagnostics & System Health

100% Local / Zero Telemetry
Local Diagnostics Recording Maintains an in-memory ring buffer of runtime errors, renderer crashes, and health logs to assist troubleshooting. Disabled by default. Zero telemetry or network transmission; all data remains strictly in volatile RAM.
